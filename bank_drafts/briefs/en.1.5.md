# 单元简报：en.1.5 现在的事

单元目标：一般现在时、现在进行时、there be、疑问句与否定句
讲解课 5 课；题库现有 0 题，目标 60 题（每课约 12 题）。

## 第 1 课（l = 1）：一般现在时　题库已有 0 题
情境：usually、often、never，还有 gets、open —— 说“经常、习惯、事实”用一般现在时。
新词：social、understand、whether、watch、together、follow、center、parent、stop、face、anything、create
搭配 / 表达：on weekends、go shopping、take a shower
- 重点：一般现在时用来说什么
  - ① 习惯、经常做的事：I walk to work. She often goes shopping.
  - ② 客观事实、真理：Water boils at 100°C. The sun rises in the east.
  - ③ 时刻表、日程（即使是将来）：The train leaves at 8 tomorrow. The class starts at nine.
  - 主语是三单时动词加 -s（上一单元讲过）：she works, it opens, he has。
  - 一句话：习惯、事实、时刻表 → 一般现在时。
  - 常错：The train will leave at 7:30.（时刻表） → The train leaves at 7:30.（时刻表用一般现在时更自然）
  - 常错：She study English every day. → She studies English every day.（三单 -s）
- 重点：频率副词放哪里：实义动词前、be 动词后
  - always 总是 &gt; usually 通常 &gt; often 经常 &gt; sometimes 有时 &gt; rarely 很少 &gt; never 从不
  - 放在实义动词前：She often eats out.
  - 放在 be 动词后：He is always late.
  - 有助动词时放在助动词后：I can never remember his name.
  - 一句话：实义动词前，be 动词后。
  - 常错：He always is late. → He is always late.（be 动词后）
  - 常错：I go often shopping. → I often go shopping.（实义动词前）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] I take a shower every night.
  - [tr] 商店九点开门。 → The shop opens at nine.

## 第 2 课（l = 2）：do / does：问句与否定　题库已有 0 题
情境：don't、does、doesn't：一般现在时的否定和疑问要“请”助动词 do 帮忙。
新词：public、already、speak、read、level、allow、add、office、spend、door、health、person
搭配 / 表达：I don't think so.、Do you know …?、What do you do?
- 重点：否定和疑问：借 do / does，动词回到原形
  - 否定：主语 + don't / doesn't + 动词原形：I don't eat meat. She doesn't like coffee.
  - 疑问：Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形？Does he work here? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
  - 三单的 -s 已经“跑到” does 上了，后面的动词就不再加 s：She doesn't like（不是 likes）。
  - 一句话：do / does 一出现，主动词就回原形。
  - 常错：She doesn't likes coffee. → She doesn't like coffee.（does 之后用原形）
  - 常错：He not work here. → He doesn't work here.（要借 doesn't）
- 重点：be 动词的句子不用 do
  - 句子的谓语是 be（am / is / are）时，否定和疑问直接用 be：Are you tired? I'm not hungry.
  - 谓语是实义动词时才用 do / does：Do you like it? I don't know.
  - 一句话：有实义动词用 do，没有就用 be。
  - 常错：Do you tired? → Are you tired?（tired 是形容词，用 be）
  - 常错：Are you like music? → Do you like music?（like 是实义动词，用 do）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [c] “你是做什么工作的？”　选项：What are you do? / What do you do? / What you do? / What does you do?　答案：What do you do?
  - [o] Do you know his name?
  - [tr] 你是做什么工作的？ → What do you do?

## 第 3 课（l = 3）：现在进行时　题库已有 0 题
情境：am / is / are + -ing：说“此刻正在发生”的事。
新词：art、sure、war、history、party、within、grow、result、open、morning、walk、reason
搭配 / 表达：at the moment、wait for、talk to
- 重点：现在进行时 vs 一般现在时
  - 现在进行时 am / is / are + -ing：此刻正在，或这段时间暂时如此 —— I'm reading now. I'm living with my parents this month.
  - 一般现在时：经常、长期如此 —— I read every night. I live in Shanghai.
  - 信号词：now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen! → 进行时；every day, usually, often → 一般现在时
  - 一句话：此刻、临时 → 进行时；经常、长期 → 一般现在时。
  - 常错：Listen! Someone knocks at the door. → Listen! Someone is knocking at the door.（此刻正在发生）
  - 常错：I am go to work every day. → I go to work every day.（习惯用一般现在时）
- 重点：状态动词一般不用进行时
  - 表示状态、感觉、想法、拥有的动词一般不用进行时：know, like, love, want, need, believe, understand, have（拥有）, belong
  - I know the answer.（不说 I'm knowing）　I want a coffee.（不说 I'm wanting）
  - 一句话：know / like / want / need / believe：不用 -ing。
  - 常错：I am knowing the answer. → I know the answer.（）
  - 常错：She is wanting a new phone. → She wants a new phone.（）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [f] I'm waiting ___ my friend.　答案：for
  - [o] Who are you talking to?
  - [tr] 我妈妈正在做晚饭。 → My mother is cooking dinner.

## 第 4 课（l = 4）：There be：某处有某物　题库已有 0 题
情境：“某处有某物”英语用 there is / there are，不用 have。
新词：low、win、research、girl、guy、early、food、himself、moment、air、teacher、force
搭配 / 表达：near here、next to、on the left
- 重点：“某处有某物”：There be，不是 have
  - There is + 单数 / 不可数：There is a park near my house. There is some milk.
  - There are + 复数：There are two banks on this street.
  - have 表示“某人拥有”：I have a car. 不能用来说“某处有”。
  - 一句话：地点 + 有 → There is / There are；人拥有 → have。
  - 常错：The table has a book. → There is a book on the table.（）
  - 常错：Here has a bank. → There is a bank here.（）
- 重点：There be 的就近原则、否定和疑问
  - be 和紧挨着的第一个名词一致：There is a pen and two books. / There are two books and a pen.
  - 否定：There isn't / aren't + any：There aren't any chairs.
  - 疑问：Is / Are there …? — Yes, there is. / No, there aren't.
  - 一句话：be 看紧挨着的那个名词。
  - 常错：There are a pen and two books. → There is a pen and two books.（就近原则）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] The café is next to the bank.
  - [tr] 咖啡馆在银行旁边。 → The café is next to the bank.

## 第 5 课（l = 5）：疑问词　题库已有 0 题
情境：what time、how often、how long：问句的“疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序”。
新词：offer、enough、education、across、although、remember、foot、second、boy、maybe、able、age
搭配 / 表达：How often …?、How long does it take?、What time …?
- 重点：疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序
  - 特殊疑问句 = 疑问词 + 一般疑问句：
  - Where do you live?　What is she doing?　When does the class start?
  - 问主语时例外，直接用陈述语序：Who called you?（不说 Who did call you?）　What happened?
  - 一句话：疑问词 + do / be + 主语；问主语时直接说。
  - 常错：Where you live? → Where do you live?（要加 do）
  - 常错：What you are doing? → What are you doing?（be 提前）
- 重点：how 的组合：how many / much / long / often / far
  - how many + 可数复数：多少个　how much：多少（不可数）/ 多少钱
  - how long：多长时间　how often：多久一次（回答 every day, twice a week）
  - how far：多远　how old：几岁
  - 一句话：频率 how often，时长 how long。
  - 常错：How long do you play tennis?（问频率） → How often do you play tennis?（）
  - 常错：How much people → How many people（）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [c] ___ is your birthday? — In May.　选项：What / Where / When / Who　答案：When
  - [o] What time do you get up?
  - [tr] 你多久去一次健身房？ → How often do you go to the gym?
