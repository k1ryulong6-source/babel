# 单元简报：en.1.8 句子升级

单元目标：被动语态、to do 与 doing、定语从句入门、宾语从句、学期总复习
讲解课 5 课；题库现有 0 题，目标 60 题（每课约 12 题）。

## 第 1 课（l = 1）：被动语态入门　题库已有 0 题
情境：was postponed、will be held、has been sent：托业的通知、邮件里满是被动语态。
新词：form、support、event、official、whose、matter、everyone、couple、site、project、hit、base
搭配 / 表达：be made of、be born、be called
- 重点：被动语态：be + 过去分词，什么时候用
  - 结构：be + 过去分词：English is spoken here.
  - 主动 People speak English here. → 被动 English is spoken here.（宾语变主语）
  - 什么时候用：① 不知道 / 不重要是谁做的（My bike was stolen.）② 想强调承受者（The meeting was postponed.）③ 正式文体、商务通知
  - 需要说出做事的人时用 by：The book was written by a young doctor.
  - 一句话：被动 = be + 过去分词；不知道谁做 / 强调承受者 / 正式通知时用。
  - 常错：This car made in Japan. → This car was made in Japan.（缺 be）
  - 常错：I born in 1995. → I was born in 1995.（）
- 重点：被动语态的时态：be 跟着变
  - 现在：is / are + done　过去：was / were + done
  - 将来：will be + done　完成：has / have been + done
  - 情态：can / must / should be + done　进行：is / are being + done
  - 一句话：时态看 be，分词不变。
  - 常错：The room hasn't cleaned yet. → The room hasn't been cleaned yet.（缺 been）
  - 常错：It will sent tomorrow. → It will be sent tomorrow.（缺 be）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] This bridge was built in 1990.

## 第 2 课（l = 2）：to do 与 doing　题库已有 0 题
情境：decided to accept、thank you for coming、look forward to hearing：一封邮件里 to do 和 doing 都出现了。
新词：activity、star、table、court、produce、eat、teach、oil、half、situation、easy、cost
搭配 / 表达：look forward to doing、be used to doing、decide to do
- 重点：意思不同的 to do / doing：stop、remember、try
  - stop doing 停止正在做的事 / stop to do 停下来去做另一件事
  - remember to do 记得要去做（还没做）/ remember doing 记得做过（已做）
  - forget to do 忘了去做 / forget doing 忘了做过
  - try to do 努力做 / try doing 试着做做看
  - 一句话：to do 还没做，doing 已经在做 / 做过。
  - 常错：Remember locking the door when you leave. → Remember to lock the door when you leave.（还没做用 to do）
- 重点：介词后面用 doing：look forward to doing
  - 介词（for, of, in, at, about, without, before, after…）后面的动词用 -ing：Thank you for coming. I'm good at cooking. before leaving
  - 这几个短语里的 to 是介词，后面也用 -ing：look forward to doing、be used to doing、object to doing
  - 一句话：介词 + -ing；look forward to / be used to 的 to 是介词。
  - 常错：I look forward to hear from you. → I look forward to hearing from you.（to 是介词）
  - 常错：Thank you for come. → Thank you for coming.（）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] I look forward to seeing you.
  - [tr] 我们决定接受这个提案。 → We decided to accept the proposal.

## 第 3 课（l = 3）：定语从句入门　题库已有 0 题
情境：the woman who called you、the phone you bought：英语用一个句子来修饰名词，放在名词后面。
新词：industry、figure、street、image、itself、phone、either、data、cover、quite、picture、clear
搭配 / 表达：the one who …、something that …、the place where …
- 重点：定语从句：中文修饰在前，英语放在后
  - 中文：给你打电话的那位女士 —— 修饰语在名词前。
  - 英语：the woman who called you —— 修饰的句子放在名词后，用关系词连接。
  - 翻译时先说名词，再用 who / which / that 引出“的”字部分。
  - 一句话：名词在前，修饰的句子在后。
  - 常错：The who lives next door man → The man who lives next door（从句放名词后）
  - 常错：I bought yesterday the bag → the bag I bought yesterday（）
- 重点：who / which / that / whose，什么时候能省
  - who 指人，which 指物，that 人和物都可以（口语最常用）
  - whose = ……的：the girl whose father is a pilot
  - where 指地点：the place where we met
  - 关系词在从句里作宾语时可以省略：the movie (that) we saw；作主语时不能省：the man who lives here
  - 一句话：后面有新主语 → 可省；没有 → 不能省。
  - 常错：The man lives next door is a doctor. → The man who lives next door is a doctor.（作主语不能省）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] She is the one who helped me.
  - [tr] 这是我读过最好的书。 → This is the best book I've ever read.

## 第 4 课（l = 4）：宾语从句与间接疑问　题库已有 0 题
情境：where the station is、if the bus is running：问句放进另一个句子里，语序就变了。
新词：practice、piece、land、recent、describe、product、doctor、wall、patient、worker、news、test
搭配 / 表达：I wonder if …、I'm not sure …、Could you tell me …?
- 重点：间接疑问：问句放进句子里，用陈述语序
  - 直接问：Where is the station?
  - 间接问：Could you tell me where the station is?（主语在前，动词在后）
  - 一般疑问变间接：Is he coming? → I don't know if / whether he is coming.
  - do / does / did 要去掉：What does he want? → I'm not sure what he wants.
  - 一句话：嵌入的问句：疑问词 + 主语 + 动词；是否用 if / whether。
  - 常错：Do you know where is the station? → Do you know where the station is?（）
  - 常错：I'm not sure what does he want. → I'm not sure what he wants.（去掉 does）
- 重点：I don't think …：否定放在前面
  - 英语说“我认为……不……”时，习惯把否定放在 think 上：
  - I don't think it will rain.（比 I think it won't rain 自然得多）
  - 同样：I don't believe …, I don't suppose …
  - 一句话：“我认为不……” → I don't think …。
  - 常错：I think he is not right. → I don't think he's right.（更自然）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [o] Could you tell me how to get there?

## 第 5 课（l = 5）：学期总复习　题库已有 0 题
情境：这一课把整个学期串起来：遇到一个句子，你应该知道先看什么、再看什么。
新词：movie、certain、North、personal、simply、technology、catch、step、baby、computer、type、attention
搭配 / 表达：keep doing、step by step、little by little
- 重点：写一个句子之前，问自己四个问题
  - ① 谓语动词在哪？（没有动词不成句：I am busy）
  - ② 时间是什么时候？（选时态：every day / now / yesterday / since / tomorrow）
  - ③ 主语是单数还是复数？（三单 -s、is / are、was / were）
  - ④ 主动还是被动？（东西不能自己做事：The email was sent）
  - 一句话：动词、时间、主语、主被动 —— 写完一句检查四问。
  - 常错：My sister work in a bank. → My sister works in a bank.（③）
  - 常错：The email sent yesterday. → The email was sent yesterday.（④）
已有题目示例（不要重复，也不要只换几个词）：
  - [c] He is the ___ student in our class.　选项：tall / taller / tallest / most tall　答案：tallest
  - [f] Keep ___ (practice) every day.　答案：practicing
  - [o] My English is getting better little by little.
